Kiwi Dash Privacy Policy

Kiwi Dash is a children's game by Michael Clark. This policy explains what the app does with information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no social features and no links to other websites or apps. It does not currently show adverts. If adverts are added in future, this policy will be updated first and they will be limited to child-safe ads.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, best score, coins, daily goals and sound setting on your own device so they are there next time. This information never leaves the device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

The app uses the internet permission only to load its fonts and, in future, child-safe adverts. It does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Children

The game is designed to be safe for children. It collects no information from anyone, including children, and contains no in-app purchases. Coins in the game are earned by playing and cannot be bought with money.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
