Kiwi Dash Privacy Policy

Kiwi Dash is a children's game by Michael Clark. This policy explains what the app does with information.

What we collect

The game itself collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics and no social features, and we do not store, share or sell any personal information about players. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, which shows adverts as described below.

Adverts

Kiwi Dash may show short adverts provided by Google AdMob, only between levels, for example after a level is cleared, and never while you are playing. Because the game is made for children, adverts are set as child-directed: they are not personalised, no advertising profile is built, and the advertising ID is not used. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages. Google may process limited technical information, such as the device type and IP address, to deliver and measure adverts. You can read how Google handles this at policies.google.com/privacy.

Removing adverts

A one-time purchase through Google Play may be offered to remove adverts. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store payment details. Coins in the game are earned by playing and cannot be bought with money.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, best score, coins, daily goals and sound setting on your own device so they are there next time. This information never leaves the device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

The app uses the internet permission only to load its fonts and to show adverts. It does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Children

The game is designed to be safe for children. The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and shows only non-personalised, child-directed adverts. Parents who do not want any adverts can use the optional remove adverts purchase.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
